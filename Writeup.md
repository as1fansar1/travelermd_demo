# Group wishlist for Lisbon: write-up

**Prototype:** https://claude.ai/artifact/Ro4xB96NhXFBvdWSFi1eg1 · **Code:** https://github.com/as1fansar1/travelermd_demo

**Competitive scan**
- **Borrow (Expedia):** "Compare stays" puts saved stays side by side. Here the rows come from what matters on this trip.
- **Weak (Booking.com):** one tap on the heart removes a stay, and a shared list doesn't show who's on it.

**Product judgment**
- **Main user: Alex, the organizer.** The job is to get four people to commit to one stay without chasing them. Alex sees each total against the budget, who is happy with which stay, and whom to nudge.
- **Biggest tradeoff: memory adds notes but never ranks.** It's a group trip, so Alex's "quiet at night" flags Central's street noise but doesn't push Central down. Friends tap every stay they'd be happy with, which shows where the group overlaps instead of deadlocking on favourites.
- **Test first: do organizers act on memory?** With 8–10 real organizers: if fewer than half open a flag or mark a preference "not this trip", memory shrinks to one heads-up per conflict.
